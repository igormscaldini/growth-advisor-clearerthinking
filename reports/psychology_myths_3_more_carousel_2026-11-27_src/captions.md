Does shouting or hitting a punching bag actually make you less angry?

Some ideas about the mind are appealing but rarely true. In our new article, Spencer Greenberg looks at three of them: that we repress traumatic memories, that finding the root cause of a mental health problem cures it, and that venting reduces anger.

The carousel covers 5 takeaways from our article, including:
• Whether traumatic memories really get repressed
• What happened when students were asked to recall the Challenger disaster about three years later
• Why one of the most studied therapies in the world doesn't focus on where a problem came from
• What tends to reduce anger when venting doesn't
