FarmKind + Clearer Thinking: sponsorship copy, Versions G and H (week of December 1)

Notes for Polina: two newsletter box versions written with the reader as the hero and FarmKind as the guide. Both frame the cause as reducing animal suffering, say nothing about diet, ask for a monthly gift, and name the result in numbers and time. The "animal supporter" call to action is our phrase; swap in your own wording if you prefer. Every figure is attributed to FarmKind or linked to its source, and every link to farmkind.giving carries our tracking parameters (utm_source=newsletter, utm_medium=email, utm_campaign=clearer_thinking), so the visits show up in your analytics; send your own tracked links if you would rather use those. Two things to check on your side: that the fee sentence matches what donors see at checkout, and whether the donate form can preselect a monthly gift.

Version G: the plan

🐔 You can reduce animal suffering this year. It takes a few minutes and about $12 a month.

By FarmKind's estimate, $12 a month to the charities it recommends spares about one animal a day from suffering, around 390 over a year. That is the whole offer: a low-cost, low-effort action you set up once, and a measurable result you can point to next December.

The scale is hard to picture. About 112 billion farmed animals are alive at any time, more than 130 times the number of pet dogs and cats, and many of them spend their whole lives in cages or crates. They do not benefit from how anyone feels about them, only from what gets funded.

Here is the plan. Pick an amount. Set it up once through FarmKind, a grant-funded nonprofit that takes no cut and chooses its six charities only from Animal Charity Evaluators' recommended list: groups that get food companies to commit to taking hens out of cages ($10 to The Humane League protects about eight hens) and improve conditions for fish and shrimp. Your gift is split across all six, and you are done for the year.

Become an animal supporter: set up your monthly gift, from $12 a month, or see where it goes first.

Version H: the transformation

🐷 Turn caring about animals into fewer animals suffering: a few minutes now, about 390 animals spared over the next year.

Other ways to help animals ask for your time, or for a research project to find a charity that works. This one asks for a few minutes and a small standing gift. By FarmKind's estimate, $12 a month spares about one animal a day from suffering, and $10 to The Humane League alone protects about eight hens from cages.

The research project is already done. FarmKind, a grant-funded nonprofit that takes no cut, picks six charities only from Animal Charity Evaluators' recommended list, and the groups it funds have a record you can check, including the campaigns behind U.S. eggs going from about 4% cage-free to around 45% in a decade and cage-free commitments from more than 300 major companies. Your gift is split across all six.

What changes is small and concrete. You set this up once, this year. Next year, by the estimates FarmKind publishes, there are a few hundred animals whose suffering you prevented, and you are a person who acted on caring about animals rather than only feeling it.

Be an animal supporter: start your monthly gift, at whatever amount fits.
