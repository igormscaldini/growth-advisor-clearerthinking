Newsletter box, Version A: offset a cheeseburger

🍔 You can offset a flight. FarmKind lets you offset a cheeseburger.

Carbon offsets exist because most people will not stop flying, and a payment that undoes the damage beats a resolution that never happens. FarmKind applies the same logic to dinner. Its Compassion Calculator takes your diet, estimates its impact on farmed animals, and tells you the donation that would prevent the same amount of suffering, modelled conservatively on purpose. By FarmKind's estimate, about $23 a month does as much good as going completely vegan.

The money goes to six charities, all picked from Animal Charity Evaluators' recommended list, so the vetting is independent. The Humane League runs corporate campaigns that get hens out of cages: $10 there protects about eight of them. The Good Food Institute works on alternative proteins. Fish Welfare Initiative and Shrimp Welfare Project cover the animals almost nobody else counts. FarmKind is grant-funded and takes no cut; apart from card fees and an optional tip to Every.org, the payment platform, which you can set to zero, your donation goes to the charities.

If you have been meaning to eat less meat since roughly 2019, this is the version of that plan that actually runs.

Donate to FarmKind's Impact Fund or try the Compassion Calculator.

Newsletter box, Version B: the 3% problem

🐟 99% of American farm animals live on factory farms. They get 3% of the animal-charity money.

The other 97% goes to shelters, rescues and wildlife, and nobody is asking you to stop. But the gap means a dollar aimed at factory farming goes a long way. FarmKind's comparison: the roughly $3,000 a shelter spends on one dog in a year could help more than 6,000 animals on factory farms.

FarmKind is a grant-funded nonprofit that routes donations to six charities, all picked from Animal Charity Evaluators' recommended list. Its Impact Fund splits one gift across all six, which FarmKind calls an investment portfolio for ending factory farming: corporate campaigns that get hens out of cages ($10 to The Humane League protects about eight), alternative-protein research, and welfare work for fish and shrimp. FarmKind takes no cut; apart from card fees and an optional tip to Every.org that you can set to zero, the money reaches the charities.

Not ready to change your diet? FarmKind's answer is to offset it instead. Its Compassion Calculator estimates the donation that balances your meals, and by their estimate about $23 a month does as much good as going vegan.

Donate to the Impact Fund or try the Compassion Calculator.
