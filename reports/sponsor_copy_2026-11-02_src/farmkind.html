Newsletter box, Version A: offset your plate

🐔 $23 a month, or go vegan. FarmKind says the two do about the same good for farmed animals.

The number is FarmKind's estimate, and their Compassion Calculator applies the same idea to your diet: pick it, and the calculator estimates its impact on farmed animals and the donation that would balance it, modelled conservatively by FarmKind's own account. They call it carbon offsetting for your plate. For a diet with meat, dairy and eggs, the answer is a monthly gift rather than a new menu.

Where that gift goes is what makes the trade credible. FarmKind's six charities all come from Animal Charity Evaluators' recommended list: The Humane League, whose main work is corporate campaigns pushing food companies to get hens out of cages ($10 there protects about eight hens), the Good Food Institute's research on alternative proteins, and welfare programs for fish and shrimp.

If that trade sounds better than a diet change you keep postponing, give to the Impact Fund this week and one gift is divided among all six. FarmKind is grant-funded and takes no cut; the only deductions are card or PayPal fees and an optional tip to Every.org, the payment platform, and the tip can be set to zero.

Donate to FarmKind's Impact Fund or try the Compassion Calculator.

Newsletter box, Version B: the 3% problem

🐟 99% of American farm animals live on factory farms. 3% of animal-charity dollars go to farmed animals.

The rest goes to other animal causes, and they deserve support. But FarmKind's argument is that where you give matters more than how much, and the 3% shows where the gap is. Their comparison: the roughly $3,000 a shelter spends on one dog in a year can help more than 6,000 animals on factory farms when it goes to the charities they recommend.

FarmKind picks its six charities only from Animal Charity Evaluators' recommended list, so the vetting is done by an independent evaluator. Among them: corporate campaigns to get hens out of cages ($10 to The Humane League protects about eight of them), alternative-protein research, and welfare programs for fish and shrimp.

$23 a month, or go vegan: by FarmKind's own, deliberately conservative estimate, the two do about the same good. Their Compassion Calculator gives the number for your own diet.

Give to the Impact Fund this week: one gift, six charities, no cut taken by FarmKind. The only deductions are card fees and an optional tip to Every.org, the payment platform, and the tip can be set to zero.

Donate to the Impact Fund or try the Compassion Calculator.
