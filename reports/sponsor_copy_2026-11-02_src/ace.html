Newsletter box

🐔 You can double your donation to help reduce animal suffering.

A donor has pledged a $300,000 matching gift to Animal Charity Evaluators (ACE), a nonprofit that uses evidence and reason to identify the charities that can help the most animals. That means every donation made from November 14 through December 7 will be matched dollar for dollar. This is a good opportunity to make your gift go further in reducing animal suffering: a $50 donation becomes $100.

So if you want to help animals, now is a very good time.
