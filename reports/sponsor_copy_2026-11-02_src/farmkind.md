## Newsletter box, Version A: offset your plate

🐔 **You can do as much for farmed animals as going vegan, without changing what you eat.**

Most people say they are against factory farming, and most of them keep eating what they ate before. FarmKind's starting point is that this is not a character flaw. In their words: "The problem isn't you. It's factory farming." So instead of asking you to change your diet, they ask you to offset it.

Their Compassion Calculator works like carbon offsetting for your plate. Pick your diet and it estimates the suffering that diet involves, then the donation that would prevent the same amount. The money goes to six charities chosen from Animal Charity Evaluators' recommended list (among them The Humane League, the Good Food Institute and the Shrimp Welfare Project), split in proportion to what you eat. By FarmKind's estimate, $23 a month does as much good as going completely vegan. $10 to The Humane League protects about eight hens.

FarmKind is a nonprofit funded by grants, so it does not take a cut: 100% of your donation goes to the charities. And the dollars go unusually far, because only about 3% of all animal-related giving reaches farmed animals.

[Try the Compassion Calculator](https://farmkind.giving/compassion-calculator) or [donate to FarmKind's Impact Fund](https://farmkind.giving/).

## Newsletter box, Version B: the 3% problem

🐟 **99% of US farm animals live on factory farms. 3% of animal-charity dollars reach them.**

Shelters, rescues and wildlife get the rest, and they deserve it. But the mismatch means a dollar aimed at factory farming goes unusually far. FarmKind puts it this way: the $3,000 a shelter spends on one dog in a year can help more than 6,000 animals on factory farms when it goes to the charities they recommend.

FarmKind is a nonprofit donation platform. It only considers charities Animal Charity Evaluators has recommended, then picks six that cover the main kinds of animals factory farming harms, from chickens and pigs to fish and shrimp. You can give to one of them or to the Impact Fund, which they describe as an investment portfolio for ending factory farming. Because FarmKind is grant-funded, 100% of what you give goes to the charities.

Curious what your own diet implies? Their Compassion Calculator estimates the donation that offsets it, like carbon offsetting for your plate. By FarmKind's estimate, $23 a month to these charities does as much good as going completely vegan.

[Donate to the Impact Fund](https://farmkind.giving/) or [try the Compassion Calculator](https://farmkind.giving/compassion-calculator).
