## Newsletter box, Version A: offset your plate

🐔 **$23 a month, or go vegan. FarmKind says the two do about the same good for farmed animals.**

That claim rests on where the money goes. FarmKind's six charities all come from Animal Charity Evaluators' recommended list, and their work is concrete: corporate campaigns that push food companies to get hens out of cages (The Humane League's main work, which is why $10 there protects about eight hens), research on alternative proteins (the Good Food Institute), and welfare programs for fish and shrimp, animals almost no donor thinks about.

FarmKind is a grant-funded nonprofit and takes no cut. The only deductions are card fees and an optional tip to Every.org, the payment platform, which you can set to zero. Give to the Impact Fund and one gift is divided among all six.

If you want the number for your own diet, the Compassion Calculator estimates the suffering your meals involve and the donation that would prevent the same amount, modelled conservatively by FarmKind's own account. It is the closest thing to carbon offsetting for your plate, and it exists because most people who dislike factory farming never change what they eat.

[Donate to FarmKind's Impact Fund](https://farmkind.giving/donate) or [try the Compassion Calculator](https://farmkind.giving/compassion-calculator).

## Newsletter box, Version B: the 3% problem

🐟 **99% of American farm animals live on factory farms. 3% of animal-charity dollars go to farmed animals.**

The rest goes to other animal causes, and they deserve support. But FarmKind's argument is that the mismatch leaves the cheapest ways to reduce suffering on farms underfunded, so a dollar aimed there buys more. Their comparison: the roughly $3,000 a shelter spends on one dog in a year can help more than 6,000 animals on factory farms when it goes to the charities they recommend.

FarmKind picks its six charities only from Animal Charity Evaluators' recommended list, so the vetting is done by an independent evaluator. The work is concrete: corporate campaigns to get hens out of cages ($10 to The Humane League protects about eight of them), alternative-protein research, and welfare programs for fish and shrimp. Not ready to change your diet? The Compassion Calculator estimates the donation that offsets it, and by FarmKind's estimate about $23 a month does as much good as going completely vegan.

If this persuades you, give to the Impact Fund this week: one gift, six charities, no cut taken by FarmKind, and an optional tip to Every.org, the payment platform, which you can set to zero.

[Donate to the Impact Fund](https://farmkind.giving/donate) or [try the Compassion Calculator](https://farmkind.giving/compassion-calculator).
