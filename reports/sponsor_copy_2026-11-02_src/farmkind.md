## Newsletter box, Version A: offset your plate

🐔 **$23 a month, or go vegan. FarmKind says the two do about the same good for farmed animals.**

The number comes from FarmKind's Compassion Calculator, which they describe as carbon offsetting, but for your plate. Pick your diet and it estimates the suffering your meals involve, then the donation that would prevent the same amount, modelled conservatively by FarmKind's own account. For a diet with meat, dairy and eggs, the answer is a monthly gift rather than a new menu.

Where that gift goes is what makes the trade credible. FarmKind's six charities all come from Animal Charity Evaluators' recommended list: The Humane League, whose main work is corporate campaigns pushing food companies to get hens out of cages ($10 there protects about eight hens), the Good Food Institute's research on alternative proteins, and welfare programs for fish and shrimp.

If that trade sounds better than the diet change you were not going to make anyway, give to the Impact Fund this week and one gift is divided among all six. FarmKind is grant-funded and takes no cut; the only deductions are card or PayPal fees and an optional tip to Every.org, the payment platform, which you can set to zero.

[Donate to FarmKind's Impact Fund](https://farmkind.giving/donate) or [try the Compassion Calculator](https://farmkind.giving/compassion-calculator).

## Newsletter box, Version B: the 3% problem

🐟 **99% of American farm animals live on factory farms. 3% of animal-charity dollars go to farmed animals.**

The rest goes to other animal causes, and they deserve support. But FarmKind's argument is that the mismatch leaves the cheapest ways to reduce suffering on farms underfunded, so a dollar aimed there buys more. Their comparison: the roughly $3,000 a shelter spends on one dog in a year can help more than 6,000 animals on factory farms when it goes to the charities they recommend.

FarmKind picks its six charities only from Animal Charity Evaluators' recommended list, so the vetting is done by an independent evaluator. Among them: corporate campaigns to get hens out of cages ($10 to The Humane League protects about eight of them), alternative-protein research, and welfare programs for fish and shrimp.

Not ready to change your diet? The Compassion Calculator estimates the donation that offsets it, and by FarmKind's estimate, which they say they model conservatively, about $23 a month does as much good as going completely vegan.

Give to the Impact Fund this week: one gift, six charities, no cut taken by FarmKind, and an optional tip to Every.org, the payment platform, which you can set to zero.

[Donate to the Impact Fund](https://farmkind.giving/donate) or [try the Compassion Calculator](https://farmkind.giving/compassion-calculator).
