## Newsletter box, Version A: offset your plate

🐔 **FarmKind's math: $23 a month does as much for farmed animals as going vegan, with no change to your plate.**

FarmKind's pitch is aimed at people who dislike factory farming and have not changed their diet. Their line is "The problem isn't you. It's factory farming." So instead of asking you to eat differently, they ask for a donation that offsets what you eat.

The ask is simple: a monthly gift to their Impact Fund, split across six charities that all come from Animal Charity Evaluators' recommended list, among them The Humane League, the Good Food Institute and the Shrimp Welfare Project. FarmKind is a grant-funded nonprofit and takes no cut; apart from card-processing fees, and an optional tip you can set to zero, your donation reaches the charities. $10 to The Humane League protects about eight hens.

Want the number for your own diet? The Compassion Calculator estimates the suffering your diet involves and the donation that would prevent the same amount, modelled conservatively by FarmKind's own account. For a typical diet it lands near $23 a month.

[Donate to FarmKind's Impact Fund](https://farmkind.giving/donate) or [try the Compassion Calculator](https://farmkind.giving/compassion-calculator).

## Newsletter box, Version B: the 3% problem

🐟 **99% of US farm animals live on factory farms. 3% of animal-charity dollars reach them.**

The rest goes to other animal causes, and they deserve support. FarmKind's argument is that the mismatch leaves the cheapest ways to reduce suffering on farms underfunded, so a dollar aimed there buys more. Their comparison: the $3,000 a shelter spends on one dog in a year can help more than 6,000 animals on factory farms when it goes to the charities they recommend.

FarmKind picks its six charities only from Animal Charity Evaluators' recommended list, so the vetting is done by an independent evaluator. They cover chickens, fish and shrimp among others, and if you are not ready to change your diet, the Compassion Calculator estimates the donation that offsets it: by FarmKind's estimate, about $23 a month does as much good as going completely vegan.

The simplest way to act on this is a donation to the Impact Fund: one gift, six charities, no cut taken by FarmKind, an optional tip you can set to zero. Card-processing fees are the only deduction.

[Donate to the Impact Fund](https://farmkind.giving/donate) or [try the Compassion Calculator](https://farmkind.giving/compassion-calculator).
