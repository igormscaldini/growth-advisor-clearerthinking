## Newsletter box, Version A: offset your plate

🐔 **FarmKind's math: $23 a month does as much for farmed animals as going vegan, with no change to your plate.**

FarmKind is built for people who dislike factory farming and have not changed their diet. Their line is "The problem isn't you. It's factory farming." So instead of asking you to eat differently, they ask you to offset what you eat.

Their Compassion Calculator works like carbon offsetting for your plate. Pick your diet and it estimates the suffering that diet involves, then the donation that would prevent the same amount, split across six charities in proportion to what you eat. All six come from Animal Charity Evaluators' recommended list, among them The Humane League, the Good Food Institute and the Shrimp Welfare Project. The model errs on the conservative side, by FarmKind's own account, and it lands at about $23 a month for a typical diet. $10 to The Humane League protects about eight hens.

FarmKind is a grant-funded nonprofit and takes no cut; apart from card-processing fees, your donation reaches the charities. The dollars go far, because only about 3% of all animal-related giving reaches farmed animals, so the cheapest ways to reduce suffering are still underfunded.

[Donate to FarmKind's Impact Fund](https://farmkind.giving/) or [try the Compassion Calculator](https://farmkind.giving/compassion-calculator).

## Newsletter box, Version B: the 3% problem

🐟 **99% of US farm animals live on factory farms. 3% of animal-charity dollars reach them.**

The rest goes to other animal causes, and they deserve support. But the mismatch means the cheapest ways to reduce suffering on farms are still underfunded, so a dollar aimed there goes a long way. FarmKind puts it this way: the $3,000 a shelter spends on one dog in a year can help more than 6,000 animals on factory farms when it goes to the charities they recommend.

FarmKind picks its six charities only from Animal Charity Evaluators' recommended list, so the vetting is done by an independent evaluator. The six cover the main animals factory farming harms, from chickens to fish and shrimp, and their Impact Fund splits a donation across all of them, an investment portfolio for ending factory farming, in FarmKind's words. FarmKind is a grant-funded nonprofit and takes no cut; apart from card-processing fees, your donation reaches the charities.

Not ready to change your diet? Their Compassion Calculator estimates the donation that offsets it, like carbon offsetting for your plate, modelled on the conservative side. By FarmKind's estimate, about $23 a month does as much good as going completely vegan.

[Donate to the Impact Fund](https://farmkind.giving/) or [try the Compassion Calculator](https://farmkind.giving/compassion-calculator).
