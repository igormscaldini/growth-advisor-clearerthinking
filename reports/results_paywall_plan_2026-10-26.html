Where to put a results paywall: UPT, Unique Traits Test, Rationality Test

Plan for Igor's approval, nothing has been changed on any tool. 2026-10-26 02:43pm

Short answer. Gate the Ultimate Personality Test between report page 5 and page 6: everything the page promises (snapshot, cluster, Big Five, Enneagram, Jungian type) stays free, and the paid part is what only CT has (Distinctive Traits, Predictions, the AI action plan) plus the existing $9 PDF folded in, free for CT+ members. For the Unique Traits Test, measure first (make the AI profile an on-demand button for four weeks), then gate the AI synthesis, not the trait results. Do not paywall the Rationality Test: it is the partner front door and its report is too thin to carry a price. On 2026 traffic the whole programme is worth roughly $6k to $35k a year, so treat it as a lever that pays off when paid traffic scales, not as the path to the $120k goal.

Why the gate sits after the email ask in every tool: the report already comes after the email step, so goal #4 (opens per campaign) and the MailList Accelerator deals (paid per subscriber) are untouched. Nothing in this plan moves the paywall ahead of the email.

2. What readers already pay for, and what they said about paywalls

Stripe, one-off $9 Personality PDF purchases by month (686 since January 2025, $5,868 net). October 2026 runs to the 26th.

  • The PDF converts when seen. About 333 people a month reach the PDF page and 37 buy, roughly one in seven. But only 43 clicks in 90 days came from the report pages themselves (0.39% of finishers); 850 of 1,494 PDF-page sessions were direct, i.e. the follow-up email. Demand exists; exposure inside the report does not.
  • The price test already ran, and it says $9 is not too high. The UPT randomises the PDF price shown on the report. Since 2025, the $9 card was clicked by 3.38% of readers who saw it (1,647 of 48,799), the $4.50 card by 2.9% (213 of 7,346) and the $19 card by 0.86% (19 of 2,211). Halving the price bought no extra clicks; doubling it cut clicks four-fold. Revenue per view is therefore highest at $9 of the points tested, and the untested band between $9 and $19 is where a gate price test should sit (the plan proposes $9 against $14). The same GuidedTrack *experiment machinery runs the gate test.
  • Competitors charge far more for less. Truity's full report is $19 to $29, 16Personalities' suites about $29 to EUR 99, the official MBTI $59.95 (third-party 2026 figures, unverified on the vendors' sites). CT's $9 anchor is low, and most of these sites gate the detail, not the type.
  • The audience warned us. In the March 2026 survey (n=539) paywalls were named as off-mission, NPS is -5, and only 31% knew CT+ existed. Buyers are 45%+ US; India is about 25% of sign-ups, about 0% of revenue. A gate that keeps the promised results free and makes CT+ worth more is defensible to this audience; a wall in front of the type is not.
  • Benchmarks. News paywalls convert 0.2% to 0.5% (anonymous visitors) of paywall hits a month for anonymous visitors and about 9.9% for known users (see the September benchmark report). Every reader who hits a CT gate is a known user: we have their email and 20 minutes of their attention. The scenarios below assume 3% to 12% of gate hits buy; the PDF page's one-in-seven is the optimistic bound for a pre-qualified audience.

8/10

Only 43 clicks reached the PDF page from the report pages in 90 days (0.39% of finishers); nearly all PDF buyers arrive from the follow-up email. Put the PDF card at the end of page 5 and page 8 with a preview image, and measure clicks before building anything.

For: About 333 people a month reach the PDF page and 37 buy (roughly one in seven), so the offer converts once seen; the problem is exposure. One afternoon of GuidedTrack edits, no payments code, no risk to the free experience.

Against: It is an upsell, not a paywall, so the ceiling is lower; it only tells you how the PDF sells, not how a locked report would.

5. Recommended plan

Phase 0 (week 1): measure and expose, no payments code

  1. UPT: add a PDF card with a preview image at the foot of page 5 and page 8 (option F) and record the click in the run data; today the card only shows on page 1.
  2. Unique Traits: turn the AI profile into a free "Generate my AI profile" button and record clicks (option U4). This is the hit-rate measurement for a later gate.
  3. Both: add paywall_seen, checkout_clicked and unlocked variables now so the baseline exists before anything is charged.
  4. Re-run the depth numbers the week before launch (refresh the slim export, rerun the builder) so the gate position is set on current readers.

Phase 1 (weeks 2 to 3): build the gate behind a flag

  1. Stripe: one product "Ultimate Personality Test: Full Report" with a $9 price and a $14 test price; metadata tool=upt. Tell data_layer._charge_bucket apart from the PDF by metadata, or the dashboard will file these as PDF sales.
  2. Two Vercel routes in this repo (same pattern and basic auth as the members-only check): POST /api/report-checkout creates a Checkout Session whose success URL is the reader's own report link plus &unlock={CHECKOUT_SESSION_ID}, with the email pre-filled; GET /api/report-unlock answers 1 for a paid session id, for an email with a prior purchase, or for an active CT+ member (reusing the member-check lookup). The GuidedTrack checkout program the team runs on Firebase is the alternative; it needs Belen or Josh and cannot carry a per-reader success URL today.
  3. GuidedTrack (report program 22897, in Igor's account): at the start of page 6, if not unlocked, show the gate card (what is inside, price, CT+ members free, "Already bought it? Enter your email"), call the checkout service, and return. On report start, verify an unlock parameter through the service and append it to the emailed and copied report links so revisits stay open. Send a receipt email with the unlocked link.
  4. Teasers: lock icons on items 6 to 8 of the page-1 contents list and a one-line note at the foot of pages 2 to 5, so nobody discovers the wall by surprise.
  5. Test end to end with ?paywall=1 only (Igor and the team), including a real $9 purchase and refund, a CT+ member, and a revisit from the emailed link.

Phase 2 (weeks 4 to 8): launch at 50%, read weekly

  1. GuidedTrack *experiment with three arms: control (no gate), gate at $9, gate at $14. Four weeks or 1,500 gate hits per arm, whichever comes first.
  2. Primary metrics: gate hits, checkout clicks, paid, revenue per report viewer. Guardrails that pause the test if they move more than 10% against control: email-submit rate, page 1 to page 2 continuation, the end-of-tool feedback rating, and the GSC click-through rate for "personality test" (weekly, mobile and desktop separately, as in the ranking plan).
  3. Decide: keep the winning arm at 100%, add the PDF email campaign's link to the unlocked report, and retire the standalone PDF card if the bundle outsells it.

Phase 3: Unique Traits, only with the Phase 0 numbers

If at least 40% of finishers click "Generate my AI profile", gate it (option U3) with the same routes, a page break before the AI section, and the trait lists untouched. Below that, keep it free and sell the PDF instead.

Rationality Test

No gate. Tighten the Cognitive Assessment card copy on pages 1 and 2 and keep the product grid. Revisit after the UPT numbers exist. Note that the in-run report program (id 2263) is not in Igor's GuidedTrack account, so any change there needs its owner.
