SPENCER: I actually have a term I call "light gassing" for when someone reinforces your false perceptions, so it's the opposite of gaslighting. Gaslighting is getting someone to doubt their correct perceptions or memories or senses. But light gassing is what friends often do. It's like, "Oh, he's such an asshole. He didn't deserve you anyway. Blah blah blah," which might be true. But when they do it when it's not true and they get to reinforce your false perceptions, that's light gassing. I think that's a big problem, and I think sometimes people don't do it on purpose. They do it because they're getting a very biased viewpoint of things, and they're trying to be a good friend. They think a good friend reinforces their friend, even if they aren't sure what they're saying is really accurate.
