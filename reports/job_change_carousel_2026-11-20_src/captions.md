## LinkedIn

Thinking about changing jobs? Before you start applying, it's worth asking a simpler question: what exactly are you hoping will get better?

Job satisfaction, moment-to-moment happiness and overall life satisfaction are different goals, and the same career move can affect each of them differently. A higher salary, for example, is more strongly linked to life satisfaction than to how you feel day to day at work.

The carousel covers six takeaways from our article, including:
• Why "is this interesting?" is a weak guide to job satisfaction
• How to tell which kinds of dissatisfaction will follow you to a new job
• How to test a career before you commit to it
• Why a fading honeymoon period doesn't mean the move was a mistake
