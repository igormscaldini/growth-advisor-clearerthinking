Reading the table

Across the 3 sponsor spots inside regular editions, between 2.5 and 7.4 readers per 1,000 unique opens clicked through to the sponsor. The one dedicated edition, written around the sponsor's own material, reached 17 per 1,000. The last column converts each placement into a cost per click at the $25 per 1,000 unique opens offered in our email, so you can compare it with your other channels. Four placements are too few to call a benchmark: results depend on the copy, the offer and the edition's topic.

Unique clicks are beehiiv's per-link counts of distinct subscribers who clicked, with automated scanners removed. Where a placement contained several links, the per-link counts are summed, so a reader who clicked two links counts twice. Unique opens are for the whole edition, including the sender-domain split halves.

3Who the readers are

Two sources: the March 2026 audience survey (sent to the newsletter list; each question shows its own number of answers) and the Clearer Thinking Paths quiz, a self-improvement planning tool on our site that 7,889 people completed between July 2023 and July 2026.

In short

Readers describe themselves first as lifelong learners and science enthusiasts. Between 18% (Paths quiz, n = 7,889) and 26% (survey, n = 154) identify as effective altruists or aspiring ones, about a third as rationalists, and 46% to 51% name having a greater positive impact on the world as a personal goal. Asked which other publications Clearer Thinking resembles, they most often named 80,000 Hours, LessWrong, Astral Codex Ten, Hidden Brain, You Are Not So Smart, The School of Life. Both samples are self-selected (people who answer surveys or complete a long quiz are the more engaged readers), so the shares are best read as upper bounds for the list as a whole.

4How the numbers are measured, and their limits

What each figure in this document does and does not capture, so you can weigh it on your own terms.

  • Unique opens are beehiiv's count of distinct subscribers who opened an edition. They are the closest measurable thing to "people who saw the message", but not the same thing: some mail apps open emails automatically on the reader's behalf (Apple Mail's privacy feature is the largest case), and not everyone who opens reads down to a sponsor spot. We have not estimated the size of either effect.
  • Verified clicks are beehiiv's per-link counts of distinct subscribers who clicked, after removing automated link scanners. They are the better measure of attention, which is why section 2 reports them.
  • Engaged readers (127,117) is a beehiiv segment of active subscribers whose lifetime open rate exceeds 40%, calculated 2026-10-06.
  • Country shares come from Google Analytics visits whose source is a newsletter link, not from subscriber records. Readers who open but never click are not represented, and a few countries block or strip analytics more than others.
  • Survey and quiz shares are from self-selected samples: 539 people opened the March 2026 survey and 132 to 181 answered each question; the Paths quiz is completed by people planning self-improvement, not a random slice of the list. Multi-select questions do not sum to 100%.
  • Recipients is the number of addresses an edition went to. It is not a count of readers and we do not suggest using it to size a placement.
  • Podcast and YouTube audiences are not covered here; their figures are in the separate channel description.
