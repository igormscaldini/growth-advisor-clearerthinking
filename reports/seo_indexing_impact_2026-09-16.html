Did the SEO fixes move indexing?

Fresh GSC URL Inspection sweep of all 233 tracked pages, property sc-domain:clearerthinking.org · 2026-09-16 4:58pm

Yes, but almost none of it is attributable to the fixes.

23 of the 233 tracked pages are now indexed. When you check when each of them started serving search impressions, 19 of the 23 were already indexed before the fixes shipped on 2 September. Only 3 first appeared in search after that date.

The one clean, provable win: the programs.clearerthinking.org sitemap, which had never been submitted to GSC, was submitted on 2 September, and 8 of its 10 tracked pages went from "URL is unknown to Google" to "Discovered". The other 5 sitemap submissions were redundant and produced nothing.

Why the other 5 sitemap submissions did nothing

The 58 tracked /tools/ pages were the big hope: 49 of them were "URL unknown to Google" despite being sitemap-listed. Today 48 of those 49 are still unknown.

The reason is that resubmitting them was redundant. https://www.clearerthinking.org/sitemap.xml is a sitemap index that has been registered in GSC since April 2025, is downloaded regularly (last on 16 Sep), and already references all five child sitemaps that were submitted on 2 September. Google had the tools sitemap the whole time. Submitting a child sitemap Google already reads adds no discovery signal, which is why programs (genuinely new) moved and www (already known) did not.

Correcting the sheet's diagnosis. The sheet attributes the 59 unknown tools pages to "suspected GSC property/sitemap-submission problem". That was wrong. They are sitemap-listed and the sitemap is read. They are unknown because Google is choosing not to crawl them: the pages are thin (about 230 words of mostly boilerplate to a crawler) and have almost no internal links pointing at them. Four of them are already flagged Soft 404, which is Google telling you the page looks empty. More sitemap work will not fix this; internal linking and page content will.
