Ultimate Personality Test: ranking plan for the next two weeks

Keyword "personality test", page programs.clearerthinking.org/personality-test.html. Built 2026-10-21 02:46pm from Search Console (through 2026-10-18), Ahrefs (SERP of Oct 17), URL Inspection, Wayback, the team repo and a local Lighthouse run.

The blunt version. #1 in two weeks is not on the table. The page ranks organic #7 in the US with 126 referring domains and a URL rating of 5; the #1 page has 5,547 referring domains and sits on a DR 86 domain, and an AI Overview, a "People also ask" box and sitelinks now stand between #1 and us. What is on the table in two weeks: get back onto page 1 on phones (lost around Sep 28), recover the clicks that went with it, and move one or two places by fixing the things only CT can fix (its own title, its own links, its own page).

Three of the ten actions below are already written or half done: the title fix has been sitting unmerged in a branch since Sep 23, the internal links are a Wix and template edit, and the PAA answers are a rewrite of a FAQ that already exists.

What happened on Sep 28

Mobile impressions for the exact query fell by two thirds in one week and have not recovered; desktop did not move. The rest of the site, the subdomain, the page itself (unchanged since Jul 31, Lighthouse mobile 100, LCP 1.8 s, crawled as mobile on Oct 21 with the right canonical) and user engagement (mobile engagement rate 95% or better all through) are all clean. The reported mobile position moved only from 8.8 to 9.5 while impressions fell 63%: that pattern is a page sitting at the edge of mobile page 1 and slipping under it, where Google stops counting impressions. Two things coincide: Google's September spam update (Sep 24 to Oct 8) and a new video-thumbnail feature on this SERP (absent Sep 15, present Oct 17). I could not separate the two; the remedy is the same either way, one or two positions.

What happened before that: the June click loss and the July title

Clicks for the query were 2,802 in May and 1,135 in July. The obvious suspect was the Jul 21 relaunch, which replaced the title "Free Scientific Personality Test: Briggs-Myers, Big 5 & More" with "Ultimate Personality Test | ClearerThinking.org". The weekly data clears it: click-through had already fallen from 1.5% to 0.5% on desktop by the last week of June, and it rose after the new title went live (0.98% in the first week). So the title fix in the Sep 23 branch is still worth shipping, but as a click lever, not as the explanation for the drop. The June loss lines up with a slip of about one position and a reported core update (May 21 to Jun 2); I cannot confirm that update from Google's own dashboard.

What to do, ranked

Score 10 means do it without question; 1 means do not. Each item names the owner, because three of them live in Josh's repo (willfind/clearer-thinking-programs-static).

8/10

1. Open the PR for the Sep 23 title fix today, with one tweak

The branch already changes the title to 'Free Personality Test: Myers-Briggs style, Big 5 & Enneagram' and reads title and description from program.yml. Open the PR to Josh now; the only tweak worth making is leading the description with the same words searchers type ('Free personality test ... 12 minutes, instant results').

In plain terms: The blue headline Google shows for the page is the single biggest thing deciding whether a searcher clicks. The current one says 'Ultimate Personality Test'; the #1 result says 'Free Personality Test'.

Strongest case for

Ten minutes of work, already written and preview-tested on Sep 23. The current title spends its 60 characters on a brand word and the site name, which Google already truncates. Every top-6 competitor leads with 'Free' or the test name.

Strongest case against

Be honest about the ceiling: the data shows the July title change did not cut CTR (CTR had already halved in June), so expect a CTR lift of maybe a third, not a ranking jump. Click-through is not a confirmed ranking signal.

Igor (PR), Josh (merge). Measure: desktop CTR for the exact query, 28 days before vs after.

2. Give the test a sitewide internal link from every programs.* tool page, and clean up the links that exist

Add one line to the shared program template (src/templates/program.liquid in the team repo): a footer or nav link 'Free personality test' to /personality-test.html. Then fix the links CT already has: the All Tools page links the OLD url with the anchor 'Start'; five personality blog posts and CT's own research PDF link the old url; three personality posts have no link at all; the UPT page itself links to no other CT page except the study.

In plain terms: Google decides how important a page is partly by how many of your own pages point to it and with what words. Right now most of CT's own links point at a redirect, use words like 'Start', or do not exist.

Strongest case for

Entirely in CT's control, ships in days, and gives the page ~150 same-domain links with the exact anchor on a DR 63 domain. Google's URL Inspection lists just one internal page as a known referrer today. The three programs.* pages that got internal links in September were the ones that progressed in the index.

Strongest case against

Internal links are a moderate signal on a head term, and Google must recrawl the linking pages before anything moves, so two weeks may show only the start of the effect.

Igor (Wix anchors, blog links, PR for the template), Josh (merge). Measure: Google's referringUrls in URL Inspection, then position.

7/10

3. Answer the 'People also ask' questions on the page, word for word

The SERP shows a PAA box right under the AI Overview on mobile. Rewrite the existing FAQ section so each H3 is one of the live questions ('Is there a truly free Myers-Briggs test?', 'What is the most reliable personality test?', 'What are the rarest personality types?', 'What's my Myers-Briggs type?', 'How do I know if I'm type A or B?') followed by a 40 to 60 word direct answer that names the test.

In plain terms: Google fills the 'People also ask' box with short answers lifted from pages. Matching the question exactly is how a page gets picked, and a PAA slot is a second spot on page 1.

Strongest case for

Content edit only, no engineering. The page already has a 1,600 word explainer, so this is a rewrite of headings and first sentences, not new writing. A PAA slot sits above the organic result on mobile, which is exactly where the UPT lost visibility.

Strongest case against

PAA ownership rotates and Google may prefer Psychology Today or 16personalities for the same answer. No guarantee, and GSC does not report PAA separately.

Igor writes, Josh deploys (template.liquid). Measure: impressions for the question queries in GSC.

What not to do

  • Do not disavow the June spam links. They are generic link-dump pages that link to thousands of sites; Google ignores them and a disavow file gains nothing. Revisit only if a manual action appears in Search Console.
  • Do not buy placements (Good Men Project and the DR 70 to 79 lifestyle sites from the September research). A paid link during an active spam update window is the one thing that can make this worse.
  • Do not rerun the Positly click experiment. 2,928 real clicks moved the ranking by zero; the 2026-09-23 report settles it.
  • Do not touch the url, canonical or redirect. Google indexes the page correctly, crawled it as mobile on Oct 21, and the 301 from personality.html is clean.
  • Do not read the 4.1M desktop impressions of Oct 2025 to Mar 2026 as lost traffic. That was a reporting artefact and this plan ignores that window.

Internal link audit (what CT controls today)

  • Google's URL Inspection knows one internal referrer for the page (the "full list of all personality types" post) and three external ones.
  • The homepage links the page with the anchor "Free Personality Test" (good) and also the old url via an image.
  • The All Tools page links the OLD url (personality.html, a 301) three times with the anchor "Start".
  • Five personality posts link the old url ("how accurate are personality test frameworks", "can you really trust personality tests", "announcing UPT 2.0", "what causes anxiety", "personality differences between men and women"); three have no link at all ("how many traits", "PersonalityMap", "gender and the tail end"). CT's research PDF on clearerresearch.org links the old url too.
  • The page itself links to no CT page except the accuracy study, and carries no structured data and no Open Graph tags.
  • The default program template (src/templates/program.liquid) is shared by every other tool page, so one footer line reaches ~150 pages.

Method and caveats

Search Console: domain property, exact query filter, impression-weighted positions, data through 2026-10-18 (Google's 3-day lag). Ahrefs: SERP overview US on Oct 17, backlink reports for the exact url, keyword overview. URL Inspection API for crawl and canonical state. Wayback Machine for the title history (seven captures, 2025-07 to 2026-10-15). The team repo for the Jul 21 launch, the 301 and the unmerged Sep 23 branch. Lighthouse 12 run locally on mobile emulation because the PageSpeed Insights quota was exhausted, so there is no field (CrUX) data here. The Ahrefs SERP capture for Sep 15 looked unreliable (news and forum results at the top) and was not used. Spam-link classification is from Ahrefs' is_spam flag and the page titles, which name the link-dump services. The backlink and memory records show no link buying by CT.
