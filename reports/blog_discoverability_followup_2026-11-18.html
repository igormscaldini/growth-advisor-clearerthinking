Blog discoverability: where Markus's findings stand

2026-11-19 02:37pm · Markus's email of 2026-07-30, re-tested on 2026-11-18 against the live site, the Wix blog and Search Console

Markus's main diagnosis was right, and the fix he suggested is the one that worked. Google now indexes 574 of the blog's 626 articles (91.7%), and 94 of the 136 posts it was skipping in August. The jump came the week the All Clearer Thinking Articles page went live (2026-09-30), not after the sitemap resubmission four weeks earlier, and Google names that page as the place it found 600 of the 626 articles.

What it did not do is make his article findable. It is indexed, but since September it has been shown 10 times and clicked 1 time. Indexing was the precondition; ranking is a separate problem that an index page cannot solve.

Of the six findings: four are closed (three by the index page, one by redirects), and two are exactly where they were in July, categories on purpose and the duplicate glossary posts through neglect.

Three things say the index page caused this and not time passing:

  • Timing. For four weeks after the sitemaps were resubmitted the line stayed at 10 posts. It tripled in the week the index page went live and reached 51 in the latest week.
  • Google's own record. Search Console lists the index page as a referring page for 600 of 626 articles.
  • First appearances. Of the 94 newly indexed posts, 59 first appeared in search after it went live, 13 before, and 22 are indexed but have not been shown to anyone yet.

The size of the win is modest. Those posts went from 1 to 27 search clicks a week (47 in the latest week, still rising), against roughly 2,322 a week for the whole blog. Whole-blog clicks moved from 2,101 to 2,322 a week over the same windows, but only about 26 of that difference can be traced to these posts. The rest cannot be credited to anything here: there is no control group.

Search Console's verdicts lag its own search data: 5 of the 52 articles it calls not indexed earned impressions in the last four weeks, so the true indexed count is probably a few above 574.

The article Markus flagged

What 20,000 People Taught Us About Entrepreneurship and Startups is indexed. Google's last crawl of it is 2026-07-30 21:07 UTC, the minute you replied to Markus, which fits an indexing request made from Search Console that afternoon and not anything done later.

Its history shows the real problem. Published in July 2025, it earned 218 impressions in its first month, faded, and then showed zero impressions for 8 straight months (Nov 2025 to Jun 2026). After the re-crawl it got 183 impressions in August at positions 8 to 20, and then faded the same way: 10 impressions and 1 click from September 1 to 2026-11-16. Search Console reports only "all people who start up a business based on a new idea are always successful." (4 impressions, average position 19) and "general entrepreneurial tendency test" (1 impression, average position 71); every other query is below its reporting threshold.

So Google has the page and is choosing not to show it. It has links from the index page and from 3 other articles (career-advice-from-conversations-with-experts, study-report-why-scams-succeed-and-cures-fail-understanding-hype-vs-value, why-scams-succeed-and-cures-fail-understanding-hype-vs-value), and no category. Whether his exact test (searching the title) now finds it could not be checked from here: Search Console has logged no title search since his email. It takes ten seconds to try by hand.

What is still open

  • 5 real articles worth a manual "Request indexing" in Search Console. 4 carry a stale "Soft 404" verdict from a crawl last spring although the pages are fine (a-better-way-to-ask-why-from-behavioral-science, decision-advisor-a-randomized-controlled-trial-of-a-decision-making-tool, how-to-understand-your-morality-using-data, why-don-t-chimps-run-the-world), and Google crawled and declined is-what-youve-been-taught-about-biases-wrong on 2026-11-14, a newsletter article from October. Ten minutes.
  • 448 articles have the index page as their only internal link. One link from a page with 626 others tells Google an article exists, not that it matters. Article-to-article linking is unchanged since July: 451 of 612 posts had no link from another article then, 457 of 626 have none now. This is the lever left for ranking, and it is editorial work: a few contextual links into each older piece worth ranking, and a related-articles block on new ones.
  • The 9 duplicate glossary posts. Unpublish each -1 copy and 301 it to the original, with the same Wix redirect call used for the tools. Half an hour. Low impact, since Google already ignores the copies, but it is the one finding with a clean fix left undone.
  • 52 articles not indexed: 19 from the "Definition, Examples and Effects" glossary, 9 announcements from 2015 to 2020, 24 others. The glossary and the old announcements are thin by Google's standards and more links will not change that: improve the few with search demand, leave or retire the rest.
  • Categories: leave them unless you want category pages for readers. Hand-tagging 605 posts would give a crawler at most 20 links per category, which the index page already provides for every post.

The findings in detail

1 and 4. The blog archive

Fetched as Googlebot, /blog exposes the three newest articles and no link to another archive page. /blog/page/2 to /blog/page/100 answer 200 with three articles each, and nothing links to them. That is the Wix feed widget drawing the list in the browser. The index page is an ordinary blog post, whose body Wix does render on the server; wix-hub-sync.yml re-syncs it with the sitemap every Monday (the last seven scheduled runs succeeded). It lists 626 articles with 0 missing and 0 stale, and Google's last crawl of it was 2026-11-18.

2. Categories

The blog has four categories (science 0, podcast 0, blog 3, research 25); 605 of 632 published posts are in none, and Markus's article is one of them. The research category has 25 posts and its page links 20 of them in raw HTML, which is why tagging was never going to carry the archive.

3. Internal links

Counting real <a href> links in the raw HTML of all 670 pages, and setting aside links present on more than half of them (the menu, and the "recent posts" strip that always shows the three newest articles): 175 articles are linked from at least one other article or page, 3 are the three newest, and 448 have the index page and nothing else.

5. The /tools/ pages

Each /tools/<slug> page was a short wrapper around a link to the real tool, so on 2026-09-30 they were redirected, not linked. Requested one at a time on 2026-11-18: 88 return 301 and 12 return 200 (85 to programs.clearerthinking.org, 1 to society.clearerthinking.org, 1 to selfhelppyramid.clearerthinking.org, 1 to meditation.clearerthinking.org). The 12 still answering 200 are the 11 slugs with punctuation, which the Wix redirect API cannot match, and one left alone on purpose because it points at a third-party page. Wix still lists all 100 in the sitemap because the dynamic page behind them exists.

Seen along the way

  • 1 sitemap URL redirects to a different post: recency-bias-definition-examples-and-effects goes to the "Recency Illusion" post, and both are listed.
  • 5 published posts are missing from the sitemap, and so from the index page, because one is noindexed and four are canonicalised to another page. One of those canonicals still points at programs.clearerthinking.org/personality.html, the old address of the personality test.
  • In the week of September 14 the number of distinct blog pages seen in search jumped from about 500 to 623 and fell back the week after: 177 pages got one or two impressions each. It coincides with the Who Attracts You wave of September 7 to 18; the cause was not established, and it does not touch the August-list posts charted above.
  • The society.clearerthinking.org sitemap still errors in Search Console (submitted in June, never downloaded). Not part of Markus's email.

How this was checked

  • Site: all 670 URLs in the blog, pages, categories and studies sitemaps, fetched on 2026-11-18 with a Googlebot user agent. Raw HTML only, nothing rendered, so a link drawn by JavaScript counts for nothing.
  • Categories and article bodies: the Wix Blog API, all 632 published posts.
  • Index status: Search Console URL Inspection for every article and every row of the August sheet (0 errors). Weekly figures: Search Analytics, web search, complete Monday-to-Sunday weeks from June 1.
  • July figures are quoted from Markus's email.
  • Independent recounts: articles linked from another article, 172 by the crawl and 169 by reading article bodies through the Wix API; index-page links, 626 in the rendered page and 626 in the API copy; posts with a category, 27 counted from posts against a category total of 28 (one post is in two); indexed articles, 574 by inspection against 533 with impressions in the last four weeks, with the 5 disagreements noted above.
  • Not done: a live Google search for the article's title, and a re-test of the 300-post scroll limit on /blog.
