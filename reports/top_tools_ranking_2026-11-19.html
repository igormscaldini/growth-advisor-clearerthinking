How this is counted

  • Unique users are the people who opened a tool's page at least once between Nov 19, 2025 and Nov 18, 2026, according to Google Analytics. Each person counts once per tool, however many times they came back.
  • Ranked: the 96 tools on the Clearer Thinking tools page that are hosted on programs.clearerthinking.org. Partner-branded copies of a tool are not counted.
  • The numbers include visitors brought by ads and viral posts, not only regular traffic. Who Attracts You? went viral three times between August and October 2026.
  • The Ultimate Personality Test is undercounted: since July 21, 2026 its page only reports visitors who press Start, and nothing was recorded from July 22 to August 10, 2026.
