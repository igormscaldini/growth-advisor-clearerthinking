How a sponsorship runs
  1. Pick a week and tell us what to promote and where to send people. Your own tracking link is best.
  2. We draft the copy for every placement (newsletter box, podcast read, video read) and send it for your approval, usually within a few days. You can edit anything; nothing runs without your sign-off.
  3. Lead time: final copy two weeks before the sponsored week, because Spencer records the podcast and video reads ahead of publication. A newsletter-only placement needs one week.
  4. Report and invoice within 30 days after the week runs, with the measured impressions per placement. The invoice never exceeds your cap. Payable by card or bank transfer.

Who reads Clearer Thinking
  • Readers describe themselves first as lifelong learners (81%) and science enthusiasts (45%); 34% call themselves rationalists and 18 to 26% effective altruists (aspiring included).
  • About half (46%) of audience surveys responders in 2026 name having a greater positive impact on the world as a personal goal; the topics they ask for most are critical thinking, psychology and philosophy.
  • Politically they lean progressive (63% left of centre, 19% right), and the readers we have asked are highly educated (73% with a bachelor's degree or higher).
  • Readers most often compare Clearer Thinking to 80,000 Hours, LessWrong, Astral Codex Ten, Hidden Brain, You Are Not So Smart, The School of Life.
  • 61% of engaged newsletter readers are in the United States, Canada or the United Kingdom (engaged web sessions from newsletter links, Google Analytics, n = 68,994).

Fine print. Clearer Thinking only promotes organizations, products and services it considers legitimate and a good fit for its readers, and sponsored placements are labelled as such. Appearing as a guest on the podcast is an editorial decision made by Spencer and is never part of a sponsorship. Unique opens are the figure beehiiv reports. As with every email platform, they include opens registered automatically by Apple Mail Privacy Protection. Reach figures are historical medians, not guarantees.
