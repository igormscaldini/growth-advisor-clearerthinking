Who reads Clearer Thinking

Audience profile for partners and sponsors · Data as of 2026-10-06 03:04pm · Every chart shows its own sample size

Clearer Thinking publishes a free email newsletter and free interactive tools on decision-making, psychology and thinking clearly. This page describes the people on the other end: who they say they are, what they are working on and worried about, and where they are. It draws on two reader surveys and on Google Analytics, and the last section explains what each source can and cannot tell you.

In short
  • Readers describe themselves first as lifelong learners (81%) and science enthusiasts (45%); 34% call themselves rationalists and 18 to 26% effective altruists (aspiring included).
  • About half (46% in the survey, 51% in the Paths quiz) name having a greater positive impact on the world as a personal goal; the topics they ask for most are critical thinking, psychology and philosophy.
  • They are mostly in the US, UK, Canada and Australia (67% of engaged newsletter visits) and read in English (89%).
  • Politically they lean progressive (63% left of centre, 19% right), and the readers we have asked are highly educated (73% with a bachelor's degree or higher).
  • Website visitors skew young: of those Google can classify, 39% on the main site and 53% on the free tools are 18 to 24, with 30% of main-site visitors over 45. Gender splits about evenly. Readers most often compare Clearer Thinking to 80,000 Hours, LessWrong, Astral Codex Ten, Hidden Brain, You Are Not So Smart, The School of Life.

Sources and limits

  • Audience survey, March 2026. Sent to the newsletter list; 539 people opened it and 132 to 181 answered each question. Respondents are self-selected (the more engaged readers), so treat the shares as describing the core readership rather than every subscriber. Multi-select questions do not sum to 100%.
  • Clearer Thinking Paths quiz, Jul 2023 to Jul 2026. 7,889 people completed its self-assessment. Also self-selected: people planning self-improvement, not a random slice of the list.
  • Career Navigation Survey, September 2026. 113 to 143 readers considering a career change; used only for education.
  • Google Analytics 4, Oct 2025 to Sep 2026. Country, language and device come from engaged visits that arrived via a newsletter link; they describe readers who click, not readers who only open. Age and gender come from Google's classified subset of visitors and are thresholded; August 2026 is excluded because a viral wave brought 1.1 million one-off visitors that month. Readers who block analytics are not counted.
