# CT house ad layout — the canonical structure every ad follows

This is the **brand-consistent ad template** CT actually uses. Match it so every generated ad looks like it belongs to the family. Derived from the reference coaching ad ("Your Goals. Your Coach. Your Plan.").

## Anatomy (top → bottom)

1. **Background:** clean **white** (#ffffff) — airy, lots of breathing room, generous margins. (Light grey #f5f5f5 or warm off-white #f9f1e3 acceptable if a concept calls for it.)
2. **Logo — TOP-CENTER:** the magnifying-glass "C" mark (dark grey with a bright blue #0885f8 inner arc) + the **"ClearerThinking.org"** wordmark in dark grey, centered at the top.
3. **Headline — large & bold, centered:** rounded geometric sans-serif (Avenir style), dark near-black #303030, with **one key phrase set in CT blue #0885f8** for emphasis. Short, punchy, 1–3 lines, tight line spacing. (Reference: "Your Goals. **Your Coach.** Your Plan.")
4. **Subhead / tagline — centered:** medium grey (#737373), lighter weight, smaller, 1–2 lines. States the offer plainly. (Reference: "One-on-one coaching through Clearer Thinking Plus".)
5. **Hero image — in a rounded-corner card:** warm, authentic, **golden-hour lifestyle photography** of real people (or a relevant product/screenshot), rounded corners, sitting in the lower-center. This is CT's default hero look: warm, human, optimistic, relatable — NOT cold or corporate stock.
6. **Accent badge (optional):** a filled **blue #0885f8 circle** overlapping a top corner of the hero image, white bold text, for a secondary selling point (e.g. "Affordable Monthly Access"). Use when there's a strong second hook.
7. **CTA button — rounded rectangle, bottom-center,** overlapping the hero image's lower edge: solid fill in **orange #f8911b OR blue #0885f8**, white bold text, action label (e.g. "Get Matched With a Coach"). Looks clearly tappable.

## Color roles (consistent across all ads)
- **Headline accent phrase:** CT blue #0885f8 (rest of headline #303030).
- **CTA button:** orange #f8911b or blue #0885f8 (pick one; orange reads as the warmer primary action).
- **Badge:** blue #0885f8 with white text.
- **Subhead:** grey #737373. **Background:** white.

## Visual tone
Clean, friendly, optimistic, trustworthy, lots of white space, rounded corners throughout, warm natural-light photography. Intelligent but approachable — never loud, cluttered, or stock-photo-cold.

## When to deviate
This layout is the **proven default and reference — not a mandatory template.** You have creative latitude to rearrange the composition, crop, and element placement when a concept is stronger for it (a full-bleed hero, an asymmetric layout, the logo in a corner, a different hero treatment, etc.). What must stay **constant is the brand, not the geometry**: the right palette (#0885f8 / #fecb02 / #f8911b / #233b47, white/off-white bg), the clean geometric sans-serif type, the **real CT logo from `Branding assets/`**, and the calm/intelligent/never-clickbait voice. The minimalist single-object editorial concepts (compass, maze) are a valid *hero-image* treatment; default hero = warm lifestyle photography. When unsure, fall back to this house layout — it always works — but don't force every idea into it.
