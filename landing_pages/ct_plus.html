Why Plus exists

A not-for-profit that charges for a few things so that everything else stays free.

  • A sharper picture of yourself.The personality report and the cognitive assessment go further than the free quizzes. The report is the thing more people buy from us than anything else.
  • A person in your corner.Navigator members get two one-on-one coaching sessions a month on a decision, a habit, or a goal that matters to them.
  • A stake in the free tools.Memberships help pay for the next round of studies and tools, and those stay free for everyone, member or not.

Clearer Thinking was founded by Spencer Greenberg to help people think more rationally and make better decisions using science-based tools, tested in more than 50 independent studies. Some readers have told us that paywalls feel off-mission, and we take that seriously. The 85+ tools, the newsletter, and the rest stay free. Plus bundles the pieces that already cost money to make, at a lower price than buying them one by one.

Before you decide

How do I cancel?
If, for any reason, you regret your decision, you can cancel your subscription by emailing info@clearerthinking.org. That is the whole process.
Do the free tools stay free?
Yes. Plus bundles the premium pieces that already cost money to produce. The 85+ interactive tools, the newsletter, and the rest of the free resources remain free for everyone, and memberships help fund the next ones.
What do all three tiers have in common?
Ad-free podcasts, over 10 hours of bonus content, a monthly email wrap-up of everything we published, and early access to tools before they go public. The tiers differ in what sits on top: the questions deck, the two assessments, and coaching.
Who is behind this?
Clearer Thinking is a not-for-profit project founded by Spencer Greenberg. The team has run over 50 independent studies and tests its tools on real people before publishing them.
