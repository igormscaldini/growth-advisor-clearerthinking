Why Plus exists

50+independent studies
85+free interactive tools
Clearer Thinking is a not-for-profit project founded by Spencer Greenberg to help people think more rationally and make better decisions using science-based tools. We've run over 50 independent studies and created 85+ interactive tools, along with a wide range of free resources.

Some readers have told us that paywalls feel off-mission, and we take that seriously. So Plus works the other way round. The free tools stay free. The membership bundles the premium pieces that already cost money to make, the assessments, the coaching, the bonus content, at a lower price than buying them one by one.

Before you decide

How do I cancel?
If, for any reason, you regret your decision, you can cancel your subscription by emailing info@clearerthinking.org. That is the whole process.
What do all three tiers have in common?
Ad-free podcasts, over 10 hours of bonus content, a monthly email wrap-up of everything we published, and early access to tools before they go public. The tiers differ in what sits on top: the questions deck, the two assessments, and coaching.
Do the free tools stay free?
Yes. Plus bundles the premium pieces that already cost money to produce. The 85+ interactive tools, the newsletter, and the rest of the free resources remain free for everyone, and memberships help fund the next ones.
Who is behind this?
Clearer Thinking is a not-for-profit project founded by Spencer Greenberg. The team has run over 50 independent studies and builds tools that are tested on real people before they are published.
