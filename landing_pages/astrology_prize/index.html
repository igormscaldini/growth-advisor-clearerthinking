    Our track record

    We test claims. Sometimes they pass.

    We are not in the debunking business. We are in the finding-out business, and we publish what we find. When we tested a bold claim about meditation, it held up better than many expected.

    2022 · Meditation

    Can an app teach the state some people call awakening?

    We ran a pre-registered study of 347 users of Sam Harris's Waking Up app. About 1 in 10 met our strictest criteria for having learned nondual mindfulness, and almost half of those called it the most important skill they had ever learned. The results were self-reported and preliminary, and we said that too.

    In the tradition of

    A nod to James Randi

    For decades, the magician James Randi kept an offer on the table: demonstrate a paranormal ability under conditions agreed in advance, and collect a prize that grew to $1,000,000. By his foundation's count, around a thousand people applied. Nobody collected, and the challenge closed in 2015.

    We think the idea deserves a comeback, pointed at a single claim. Like his, our test was shaped with the people who make the claim, and the pass mark is fixed before anyone plays. Unlike a debate, it ends with a number.

    This prize is run by Clearer Thinking and is not affiliated with the James Randi Educational Foundation.

    Fair questions

    Before you ask

    Is the money real?

    Yes. The first person to match at least 11 of 12 in both rounds receives $100,000.

    Why 11 of 12? Is that bar too high?

    It is the same bar we set in 2024. It also sits close to where experienced astrologers told us they already perform: after our last test, the most experienced group believed they had matched about 10 of 12. A blind guesser reaches 11 about once in 5 million tries, so nobody can call a win luck.

    I am not a professional astrologer. Can I enter?

    Yes. Anyone can take the test, and the prize goes to the first person who passes both rounds, whatever their training.

    Which kind of astrology does the test use?

    Western astrology. Every chart is shown in two house systems, Placidus and Whole Sign, the two most widely used. Our 2024 study did not cover Vedic, Chinese or other traditions, and neither does this prize.

    People describe themselves badly. Is that information good enough?

    We asked astrologers what they would want to know about a person in order to recognize their chart, and built the questions from their answers. In 2024, more than half of the astrologers finished the test believing they had beaten the top score anyone actually reached, which suggests they felt they had enough to go on.

    Are you just out to embarrass astrologers?

    No. We want to know what is true. When we tested a bold claim about meditation in 2022, the results were encouraging and we said so. If someone wins this prize, we will report that just as loudly.

    Prove us wrong. We will pay for the privilege.

    $100,000 is waiting for the first astrologer who can match 11 of 12. If you know someone who reads charts, they should hear about this from you.
