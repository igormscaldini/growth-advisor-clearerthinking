Email open rate — Clearer Thinking (beehiiv)

Each point is one email send, Jan 7 – May 27 2026. Bubble size ∝ recipients. Dashed line = audience-weighted trend.

Open rate varies mostly by send type, so points are tiered by audience size rather than shown as one line. “One Helpful Idea” notes and feature articles go to the full list (~250k–430k) and open at ~24–46%. Small operational sends (workshop reminders, internal welcomes, 9–740 recipients) hit 55–89% open rates but are off by default — they're noise for trend purposes. Weighted trend = monthly average open rate weighted by recipients across the visible tiers.
