🐔 Billions of animals suffer in silence. Here is how you can help change that.

Billions of animals have almost no one on their side: the hens trapped in cages on factory farms, and the young animals who die in the wild before they've really had the chance to live.

Facing up to the scale of this suffering can be overwhelming, and so can working out where a donation will actually make a difference.

Animal Charity Evaluators (ACE) is a nonprofit that uses evidence and reason to identify the charities that can help the most animals. Its Greatest Needs Fund directs donations to wherever funding is most needed at the time.

Thanks to a donor who pledged a matching gift, donations to the fund will be matched dollar for dollar up to $300,000 starting November 14th.

If you care about animals and aren't sure where to donate this year, this is a great opportunity to make your gift go further.
