💘 Done with dating apps? There is a swipeless way to meet someone serious.

Dating apps make money when you keep swiping, not when you leave happily paired off. Settled is a matchmaking service for people looking for serious relationships. They are building a new way of dating, with no profiles, no swiping, no ghosting.

How it works: You fill out a compatibility questionnaire, and Settled matches you with others and sets up the first date. Dates are blind by design (how good someone looks in a photo says surprisingly little about whether you will enjoy an evening together).

Joining the database takes about five minutes and is free. There are also paid tiers for those who want active matchmaking and coaching. The people on it skew intellectually curious and value-driven, and include many from the effective altruism and rationalist communities.

84% of their clients report the dates are a good use of time. 78% want to see their match again. 72% get into a serious relationship after 6 months.
