🌍 A teenager you know could be doing research on a global problem by next summer. For free.

Non-Trivial is a free online research incubator for 14-to-20-year-olds who want to make a difference in the world and have not started university. Each year, hundreds of talented students learn how to choose a problem worth working on, then 200 selected Fellows spend eight part-time summer weeks doing original research with a dedicated facilitator, weekly 1:1 calls, project grants of up to $1,000, and a share of more than $75,000 in scholarships and prizes. There are no fees at any stage.

The problems are real ones. Fellows have worked on AI governance, biosecurity, global health, pandemic preparedness, and animal welfare, and their outputs include a report co-authored for the United Nations, a paper in Nature Communications, and papers on arXiv. Since 2022, Non-Trivial has received more than 35,000 applications and awarded over $400,000. Last summer's 20 speakers included Steven Pinker, Simon Singh, and Ajeya Cotra.

Two ways to help. If you know a 14-to-20-year-old who asks big questions, forward this to them or to their parents: the application takes under an hour, and the final deadline is January 4.

If you teach, coach, or mentor teenagers, nominate a student in a few minutes, and Non-Trivial will invite them to apply.
