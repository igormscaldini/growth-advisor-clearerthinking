Hi there,


You both said yes to an accountability partner when you signed up for Clearer Thinking's Overcoming Procrastination workshop, so this email introduces the two of you. You're both on it, so you can reply to each other (no need to keep me in the loop, unless you want to!).


First, the most important part: your safety comes first. If anyone ever makes you uncomfortable, just stop replying and let us know by replying to this email (only to us, not reply-all). We'll remove them from future pairings.


Also, keep in mind it’s just an introduction. There's no obligation to do anything with it. If you'd like to check in with each other, great. If this month is busy, it's completely fine to let this email sit. Nobody is keeping score, and you won't let anyone down.


Here's an easy way to start:


1. Reply with a quick hello and, if you're comfortable, one small thing you'd like to get done in the next two weeks, and what challenges you foresee in working on this project.
2. Pick a day to check in (you both can decide on the best channel).
3. When you check in, celebrate any progress, however little. If something didn't happen, get curious together about what got in the way, without blame.


Here are some questions that might help create a productive check-in…


* What are you working on, and why does it matter to you?
* What's the smallest next step you could take this week?
* When do you usually get stuck, and what tends to get you going again?
* What kind of check-in would actually help you: a gentle reminder, a question, or just some encouragement?
* What would a good month look like for you?


Tips for being a great partner


* Ask before advising. "What's your next step?" usually helps more than suggestions nobody asked for.
* Agree on the tone. Some people like a firm nudge, others prefer a light touch. Ask which your partner prefers.
* Be gentle about misses. Everyone slips. Criticism makes the next start harder; encouragement makes it easier.
* Keep it short. A quick message counts. You don't need calls or long updates unless you both want them.
* Respect boundaries. Keep the conversation about your projects, and don't ask for personal details your partner hasn't offered.
* It's fine to stop. If it isn't working for you, you can simply say so, or just not reply. No explanation needed.
